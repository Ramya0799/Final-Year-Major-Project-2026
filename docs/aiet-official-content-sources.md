## Verified content available for the first release

The official site identifies **Alva's Institute of Engineering & Technology** as a unit of Alva's Education Foundation in Moodubidire and lists the campus address as **Shobhavana Campus, Mijar, Moodbidri – 574 225, Mangalore, DK, Karnataka, India**. The public contact page lists `principalaiet08@gmail.com` and office phone numbers, which may be presented through an accessible campus-contact card.

The public facilities page explicitly names the **Central Library and Digital Resources**, hostels, food court, ATM facility, auditoriums, health and life support, learning support, sports and fitness, gym, transport, and campus Wi-Fi. It also describes named public spaces such as the **Vivekananda Seminar Hall** and **open-air amphitheater**. No authoritative building coordinates, floors, room numbers, or GLB/GLTF models were found; these fields remain marked **Information to be updated**.

The official department listing currently includes Computer Science and Engineering (DS); Computer Science and Engineering (IoT & Cyber Security including Blockchain); Computer Science and Design; Agriculture Engineering; Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning; Information Science & Engineering; Civil Engineering; Mechanical Engineering; Training & Placement; and MBA Programme. These academic labels may be linked to a general Academic Block only where PathFinder's existing configured route data already supplies a safe location anchor.

Therefore, even though the images show indoor library spaces, they are **not evidence for indoor routing**, floor-plan overlays, map-marker placement, or 3D geometry. The published media is appropriate for visual discovery cards and gallery sections labelled at that same high level. It must not be used to assert the exact identity of a depicted building, a hostel block, a library floor, a route, or an accessible entrance unless a separate official source explicitly supplies that mapping.

The selected visual review confirms that the library image captioned **Main Reading Hall** depicts a student study space with shelving and individual desks, while the selected **College Campus** image is an aerial campus overview. PathFinder will use these only with conservative, caption-led alternative text and a link to the source page, not as a spatial representation of the walking map or 3D scene.
